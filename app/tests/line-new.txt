# LFO held on either LINE page: the line's phrase drawn anew as the next bar begins, the page stays; tapped there it
# turns to the other LINE page as it is let go. From any other page LFO goes to LINE as it is pressed, held or not.
# COLD SIGNAL, 152: a bar is 1.58 s. "phrases": the line's phrases drawn whole (the start draws the first).
listen
wait 600
expect playing 1
expect phrases 1
tap LFO
wait 50
expect page 6
# held on LINE: the page stays, and from the next bar a new phrase
said LINE
press LFO
wait 700
expect page 6
release LFO
wait 50
expect page 6
said NEW PHRASE
expect phrases 1
wait 1700
expect phrases 2
# tapped: LINE SEQ, no new phrase
tap LFO
wait 50
expect page 7
wait 1700
expect phrases 2
# held on LINE SEQ: the same
press LFO
wait 700
release LFO
wait 50
expect page 7
said NEW PHRASE
wait 1700
expect phrases 3
# tapped again: LINE
tap LFO
wait 50
expect page 6
# from MAIN, held: LINE at once, no new phrase
tap HOME
wait 50
expect page 0
said MAIN
press LFO
wait 50
expect page 6
wait 650
release LFO
wait 50
expect page 6
unsaid NEW PHRASE
wait 1700
expect phrases 3
# LINE SEQ's Density, Range and Slide act at once on the same phrase (no phrase drawn anew): fewer steps, lower notes,
# fewer slides; back where they were, the phrase as it was. (The values: COLD SIGNAL's phrase as drawn here.)
tap LFO
wait 50
expect page 7
# Mutate (K2) 0 first: nothing rewritten meanwhile
turn K2 -25
wait 30

turn K1 25
wait 30

wait 30
