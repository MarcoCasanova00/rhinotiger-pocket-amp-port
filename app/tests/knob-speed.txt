# The knobs' speed (as on the device: "knobs live"): a wide knob (0..100, FEEDBACK's 0..90) moves one a detent turned
# slowly, two a detent under 80 ms apart, four under 35; the narrow ones one a detent however fast. MAIN's DRIFT
# (param3, 50) and GROOVE's ROOT (param5, 0).
wait 2400   # the logo (face, characters, all): silent, the play begins as it goes
knobs live
wait 600
expect page 0
expect param3 50
# slowly: one a detent
turn K4 1 300
expect param3 51
turn K4 3 300
expect param3 54
turn K4 -2 300
expect param3 52
# quicker (60 ms apart): two a detent but the first (the knob still)
wait 400
turn K4 4 60
expect param3 59
# fast (20 ms apart): four a detent but the first
wait 400
turn K4 -5 20
expect param3 42
# a narrow knob, fast: one a detent
tap HOME
wait 30
expect page 1
turn K2 3 20
expect param5 3
# a spin (a script's, all at once): one a detent
tap HOME
wait 30
spin K4 5
wait 30
expect param3 47
