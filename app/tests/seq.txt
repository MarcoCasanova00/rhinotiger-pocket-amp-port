# The SEQ button: a part's pattern. Tapped (under 500 ms, acts on release) on LINE it opens LINE SEQ (page 7), again it goes
# back to LINE (6); on a part with no pattern yet it stays and says so; from a page without a part (MAIN, GROOVE, PHASE,
# ECHO, GLOBAL) it acts for the part last shown (LINE at the start). Held from 500 ms the pattern is drawn anew (LINE only)
# and the release does not also navigate. LINE SEQ's knobs: K1..K4 Density (param33), Mutate (param35), Range (param40),
# Slide (param41); ALGO there is the line's type.
listen
wait 600
expect page 0
# from MAIN, nothing shown yet: the last part is LINE
tap SEQ
wait 50
expect page 7
tap SEQ
wait 50
expect page 6
tap SEQ
wait 50
expect page 7
# LINE SEQ's knobs: K3 is Range (param40) and nothing else
expect param33 50
expect param35 50
expect param40 50
expect param41 50

wait 30
expect param40 30
expect param33 50
expect param35 50
expect param41 50
expect param32 70
expect param34 50
expect param42 50
expect param43 50
turn K1 4
turn K2 -4
turn K4 6
wait 30
expect param33 58
expect param35 42
expect param41 62
expect param40 30
# ALGO on LINE SEQ is the line's type
expect line_type 0
turn ALGO 2
