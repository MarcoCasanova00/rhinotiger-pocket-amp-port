# The builds' kinds: ALGORITHM on the PHASE page (SEQ) chooses ROLL, KICK, FILTER, TRIPLET (or AUTO); ARP from the
# groove, a build each (4 bars), then the drop. Recorded (build/host/build-kinds.wav) to listen to, a look at each.
# COLD SIGNAL, 152: a bar is about 1.58 s.
jack in
wait 1500
tap SEQ
wait 60
expect page 2
expect buildset 0
expect build 0
listen
wav build-kinds.wav
turn ALGO 1
wait 30
expect buildset 1
said BUILD ROLL
tap ARP
wait 1700
expect section 2
expect build 1
wait 3200
shot build-roll.png
wait 3400
expect section 0
wait 3200
turn ALGO 1
wait 30
expect buildset 2
tap ARP
wait 1700
expect section 2
expect build 2
wait 3200
shot build-kick.png
wait 3400
expect section 0
wait 3200
turn ALGO 1
wait 30
expect buildset 3
tap ARP
wait 1700
expect section 2
expect build 3
wait 3200
shot build-filter.png
wait 3400
expect section 0
wait 3200
turn ALGO 1
wait 30
expect buildset 4
tap ARP
wait 1700
expect section 2
expect build 4
wait 3200
shot build-triplet.png
wait 3400
expect section 0
wait 3200
wavstop
