# AUTO PLAY (SEQ's third page): a set of 30 minutes, number 7. What it turns is the hand's a while once touched, and the
# settings (what SAVE keeps) stay the hand's. Off: the music goes on from where it is. Run out: it fades and rests.
wait 3000   # the logo, then the play
jack in
listen
tap PLAY
wait 200
expect playing 0
expect setworld 2
tap SEQ
wait 30
tap SEQ
wait 30
tap SEQ
wait 30
expect page 4
turn K2 -5
wait 30
turn K3 7
wait 30
# on, resting: the set waits at its beginning for PLAY
turn K1 1
wait 30
said AUTO PLAY PLAY: BEGIN
expect autoplay 1
expect chapters >3
expect chapters <7
# its minutes and its number: only while off
turn K2 1
wait 30
said AUTO PLAY IS ON
tap PLAY
wait 8000
expect playing 1
expect setworld 2
expect section 0
# its breaks: the set's (BREAKS 0 in the engine, the hand's 50 kept)
expect engine36 0
expect param36 50
# Dark by hand: kept as it was turned for 32 bars (about 50 s), then back
tap HOME
wait 30
expect page 0
turn K3 -50
wait 30
expect engine2 0
expect param2 50
wait 30000
expect engine2 0
wait 60000
expect engine2 >10
expect param2 50
# a break by hand, and a build: followed, the drop, the set goes on
tap SAVE
wait 2500
expect section 1
tap ARP

expect section 2

expect autoplay 1
# SAVE on GLOBAL keeps the hand's
tap GLO
wait 30
tap SAVE

expect param2 50
expect setworld 2
# off: the music goes on, the knobs where the set left them
tap SEQ

tap SEQ
wait 30
tap SEQ

expect page 4

# and everything is the hand's again
