# The sections, cued with SAVE and ARP (side by side): SAVE, a break from the next bar (in a break or a build: nothing);
# ARP tapped, a build from the next bar (from the groove or a break), then the drop (4 bars); ARP tapped in a build, the
# drop at the next bar; ARP held, the drop at the next bar. Recorded (build/host/sections.wav) to listen to.
# COLD SIGNAL, 152: a bar is about 1.58 s.
wait 2400   # the logo (face, characters, all): silent, the play begins as it goes
jack in
listen
wait 600
expect section 0
wav sections.wav
# 12 bars of the groove
wait 19000
tap SAVE
said BREAK NEXT BAR
wait 1700
expect section 1
shot break.png
# SAVE in a break does nothing, and says so
tap SAVE
said IN A BREAK
wait 1700
expect section 1
# 6 bars of the break (a few spent), ARP: its build
wait 6000
tap ARP
said BUILD NEXT BAR
wait 1700
expect section 2
shot build.png
# SAVE in a build does nothing, and says so
tap SAVE
said BUILDING
wait 500
expect section 2
# the build's 4 bars, then the drop
wait 5700
expect section 0
expect drops 1
# a build straight from the groove (8 bars after the drop): ARP tapped, the build from the next bar, then the drop
wait 12600
tap ARP
said BUILD NEXT BAR
expect section 0
wait 1700
expect section 2
wait 6400
expect section 0
expect drops 2
# ARP tapped in a build: the drop at the next bar
wait 12600
tap ARP
wait 1700
expect section 2
wait 1600
tap ARP
said DROP NEXT BAR
wait 1700
expect section 0
expect drops 3
# ARP held in the groove: the drop at the next bar
wait 12600
press ARP
wait 700
said DROP NEXT BAR
release ARP
wait 1700
expect drops 4
expect section 0
wait 6000
wavstop
