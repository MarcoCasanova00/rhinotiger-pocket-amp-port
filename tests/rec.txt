# REC is the keys' loop: tapped, it is armed, and from the next bar the keys' throws are taken (each at its tick),
# four bars at most; tapped while taking, the take ends with that bar, the loop as long as the bars till then. Then it
# plays again each time round. Tapped while armed: not armed. Held: the loop gone. PLAY's rest clears it.
